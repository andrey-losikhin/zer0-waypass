# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive and specific.
- Welcome questions and different levels of experience.
- Focus criticism on ideas and code, not people.
- Respect privacy. Never request or publish credentials, decrypted password
  data, private keys or identifying store contents.
- Accept maintainer decisions and disengage from unproductive conflict.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sustained disruption, doxxing
and publication of others' private information are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns to
[andrey.losikhin@gmail.com](mailto:andrey.losikhin@gmail.com); do not publish
personal details in an issue. Reports are reviewed confidentially. The
maintainer may edit or remove content,
reject contributions, issue a warning, temporarily restrict participation or
permanently ban a participant, depending on impact and context.

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone publicly represents the
project. It is based on the principles of the Contributor Covenant 2.1.
